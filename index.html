What is midee

A library of Ableton Racks and MIDI, built from tracks we love

7,000+ Racks and MIDI with full settings inside. They work as a plugin inside Ableton and in your browser.

01

Break down tracks you love

We take apart the techniques of techno tracks producers love. You see production in action, with every setting open.

02

Make your own

Then we make our own sounds with those techniques. Change the notes and the settings, and build your track from there.

03

Grow the library together

We explore new trends and the established canons. The library grows from what the community picks.

Any BPM, any key. Sounds follow your project tempo, and you can transpose them in the plugin.
