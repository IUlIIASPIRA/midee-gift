For schools and teachers

Questions from schools and teachers

What problem does midee solve for students?

Beginners often open Ableton to an empty set and a manual. With midee they start from a working project. Every Rack opens with its MIDI and full settings, so they learn how a sound is built by changing it.

Do students need Ableton Live Suite?

For most Racks, yes. Most use instruments that come with Live 12 Suite, like Operator, Wavetable and Analog. In Intro or Standard those Racks won't open.

If your school runs another edition, write to hello@midee.me and we'll check which projects fit.

Does it work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. The midee plugin is a VST3 plugin for Ableton Live, with a Mac installer and a Windows installer. You can also browse and listen to the library in a web browser.

How do students get access?

Each student makes a free account on midee.me and installs the plugin. The free projects and free Racks work with the free account. The full library comes with a midee membership.

What does it cost after a pilot?

Let's talk: hello@midee.me. Tell me about your school and your classes.

Can I use it in class?

Yes. Open a project on the class computer, then mute, solo and change parts together. Students can open the same free project at home with a share link.

For a setup for your whole group, write to hello@midee.me.

How is it different from sample packs?

A sample is a finished audio file. A midee Rack is an Ableton instrument with its MIDI, so you can change every note and every setting. Racks follow your project tempo, and you change the key in the plugin.

Who makes the sounds?

The midee team, in Ableton Live. We break down techniques of tracks we love, then make our own sounds with them. The library grows from what the community picks.
